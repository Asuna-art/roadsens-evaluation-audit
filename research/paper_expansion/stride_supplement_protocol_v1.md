# STRIDE supplementary recording-type experiment

Frozen2026-10-16 after archive metadata/labels were audited and before any STRIDE model scores. This supplement is a different task, not the RoadSens three-class benchmark or RoadSens-to-STRIDE transfer. Official data Figshare25460755, local archiveSHA256475853a7aaf9a15068dc9c17014f3e46197acc4b705cd3851b84692cdd82f851, CC BY4.0. See external_data_feasibility.md for label and independence limits.

Population: the17 anomalyrecord folders,9Bump/8Pothole, eachfolder is one labeled example. All use the same POCO X2 deviceID; no vehicleID or road-normal labels. Labels come onlyfrom foldertype, mean recording type, not groundtruth for every sample/window. Six driving-behavior records are excluded; Standard is never renamed roadNormal. No window-level labels are generated.

For eachwhole recording, independently read Accel/Gyro x/y/z finite rows, derive3D vectornorm perstream and summarize mean/std(ddof0)/RMS/range/absmax. This gives10 whole-record offline features. No timestamps, duration, foldername or metadataID enters this feature set. Unmatched asynchronous streamtimestamps need not be interpolated since whole-record summaries are separate. Units/columnnames must be reported; do not claim shared RoadSens feature alignment. Allrecord statistics are the observation being classified; this is offline bagclassification, not a causal streamingdetector.

Use StratifiedKFold5 withfixedseeds42/43/44 on the17recording observations. Bothclasses must appear in everytrain/testfold, else stopwithoutsearchingseeds. Models: RF100 n_estimators100,depth5,min_leaf2,sqrt,balanced,seed=splitseed,n_jobs4; and training-median-imputed StandardScaler+LogisticRegression C1,lbfgs,max_iter2000,tol1e-4,balanced,seed=splitseed. No tuning on17records. Two explicit feature sets: sensor_norm_statistics10; duration_only1 (source duration from finite elapsed timestamps), with the latter onlyan acquisition-context diagnostic. Allpreprocessing trainonly. Samefolds allmodels/features, total2models×2sets×5folds×3seeds=60fits. Majority-prior dummy optionalzerocost baseline using traininglabels only.

Report eachrecord'sOOF prediction, foldmean andpooledF1/BA/perclassrecall for eachseed; do not silentlycombine them into larger n. Smalln17 makesuncertainty wide andclassstratification artificial; no significance/power/deployment claims. Duration predictivevalue may indicatecollectiondifferences butdoesnotprove causation. A nullresult is retained; do notexpandmodels orselectrecords to forcepositivefindings. No assertion of crossvehicle, crossregion or physically independentacquisition follows fromdistinctfiles oranotherFigshareDOI.

Exclusive outputdirectory, sourcearchive/memberhashes, extractioninventory, featuretable, exactsplitindices, modelconfig/code/protocolhashes, warnings/failures and compactOOFpredictions are required. Use ZIP members safely and stream necessaryCSV only; no fullarchive extraction needed. This supplement cannot satisfy a missing externallyverified three-class road-normal labeldefinition andmust be labeled as a small recording-type feasibility experiment in thepaper.
