Executed source and reproduction scope (v18)

1. The files here preserve the bytes actually executed in the original Windows workspace. Several have an absolute BASE constant. A new installation must configure an isolated working copy of those paths before building new artifacts, and record its changed source fingerprints. Do not edit or overwrite a frozen authority run.
2. Obtain Daten2018.zip from DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1461243 (CC BY 4.0). Verify MD5 e9a4eb2da043c1eb3dbf57134cf8796b and SHA256 f7f7cc6b017977d2019f1eaaf9e90d6ad6132506965bafba03c12e6b053845d3. The extractor expects it at ../independent_evidence_20261030/kit_acquisition/Daten2018.zip, relative to this extension directory.
3. Run kit_audit/extract_kit_verified.py for raw extraction, decoded GPS dates and source-overlap metadata. Run kit_build_features.py. Outputs are deliberately excluded from this repository. The feature and source audit scripts reject/record invalid labels, undated files and unsuitable windows.
4. Read kit_protocol_v1.json before any prediction work. kit_nested_runner.py --preflight performs split/budget/buffer preparation only. kit_fit_frozen.py --run is the separate training driver and consumes the saved split map; do not use the preflight draft's unused --run placeholder. Choices are saved before final predictions. The final date and all thresholds stay fixed.
5. verify_kit_features.py, verify_kit_splits.py and verify_kit_run.py check independent reconstruction and saved memberships. The split verifier also compares the original fit-free incomplete preflight authority to the completed one; that historical diagnostic authority is not in the public distribution. Preserve exact authorities for a complete original-run replay, or document an adapted verifier in a fresh replication.
6. compute_kit_events.py provides evaluate(test_indices,y_pred,data,raw,tolerance). The executed run's full-development selected predictions were evaluated at 0 and 0.5 seconds. verify_kit_events.py independently reconstructs matching and exposure from saved predictions and tests matching edge cases.
7. The PVS diagnostic consumes the already frozen primary PVS feature/selection/prediction authorities. It does not fit or alter those models. Read pvs_event_protocol.json and the explicitly post-hoc pvs_event_reporting_amendment.json. The latter separates redundant event-associated notifications from non-event alarms while retaining total unmatched counts.
8. Compact public views do not carry full inputs or exact split arrays and are not interchangeable with hash-guarded authorities. Local successful execution, independent verification and compiled manuscript PDFs are recorded; portable one-command execution on another machine was not tested.
