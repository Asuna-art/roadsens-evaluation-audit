\section*{Editorial changes}
The title, introduction and discussion now centre on classification-based pavement screening and evaluation across acquisition conditions. The article separates screening labels from roughness, severity and structural-condition measurements. Repeated disclaimers, internal simulated-review history and implementation diary passages were removed or condensed. One non-data contribution/claim matrix is retained only in the editorial archive. Five core tables appear in the article; six former main-text control tables now appear in Supplementary Section S15. Historical numerical values and contrary results are retained. The independent Karlsruhe date holdout and labelled-event diagnostics are reported with their source, grouping and exposure denominators.

The PVS primary criterion, disjoint-window increases in estimation MAE, common-budget diagnostic and identical Mexico selections remain explicit. Definitions and acquisition counts are given where needed for interpretation, with the evidence scope discussed once. The framework architecture remains an optional standalone vector asset; it is not assumed to be a required graphical abstract.
